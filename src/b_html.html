How to play

  • Drag on the left half of the screen to move.
  • Tap the yellow stick to auto-aim, or drag it to aim yourself.
  • Deal damage to charge your super, then use the star button.
  • Hide in bushes to vanish from enemies who aren't close.
  • Close-range rumblers have a zone around them. While an enemy is inside it, their super slowly charges.
  • Opponents get tougher as your trophies go up: Rookies under 300, Skilled from 300, Experts from 500 and Pros from 1,000.
  • In Showdown, finishing in the top 3 counts as a win.
  • Get the most takedowns in a match to be Star Player: +5 trophies, even if you lose.
  • On a computer: WASD to move, click to attack, E or Space for super.

Save code
